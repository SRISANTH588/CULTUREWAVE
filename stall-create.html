Stall terms and refund policy

Stall participation terms
1. The application fee reserves a review for the selected stall slot; payment does not guarantee acceptance. The admin will review your details and notify you of the decision.
2. Food stalls, food preparation, food sales, and beverage sales are not allowed under this stall program. Applications proposing food or drink may be rejected.
3. Provide accurate contact and stall information. The event admin may request clarification before deciding.
4. If accepted, follow the event's venue, access, setup, safety, timing, and conduct instructions. Do not transfer or sublet the stall without written approval.
5. CultureWave or the event organizer may decline or remove a stall that breaches these terms or venue requirements.

Refund policy
If the application is rejected, the stall fee will be refunded to the original payment method. If accepted, the fee is non-refundable if you cancel or do not attend. If the event is cancelled by its organizer, the stall fee will be refunded to the original payment method. Refund processing time depends on the payment provider and bank.
