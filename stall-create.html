Stall participation agreement

1. The fee submits your application for review. Payment does not guarantee acceptance; the event admin will review your details and contact you with the decision.
2. Food stalls, food preparation, food sales, and beverage sales are not allowed. Applications proposing food or drinks may be rejected.
3. You confirm that the information you submit is accurate and that you are authorised to represent the stall or business.
4. If accepted, follow all event venue instructions for access, setup, safety, timing, cleanliness, and conduct. Do not transfer, share, or sublet the stall without written approval.
5. The event organiser or CultureWave may decline or remove a stall that breaches these terms or venue requirements.
6. Bring and use only the equipment approved by the organiser. You are responsible for your equipment, products, staff, and personal belongings.
7. Keep your stall area clean and safe throughout the event. Dispose of packaging and waste only in the designated areas.
8. Do not block aisles, entrances, emergency exits, neighbouring stalls, or accessibility routes. Follow instructions from venue and event staff promptly.
9. Arrive and finish setup within the time given by the organiser. Do not begin trading before approval or remain in the venue after the permitted time.
10. You are responsible for any licences, permissions, insurance, taxes, and legal requirements applicable to your business and products.
11. Treat attendees, other stall holders, venue staff, and organisers respectfully. Harassment, unsafe conduct, misleading claims, or prohibited goods may result in removal.
12. The organiser may make reasonable changes to stall placement or event operations for safety, venue, or accessibility reasons and will inform you where possible.
13. The fee covers only the stall space described for this event. Furniture, power, water, internet, storage, parking, and other facilities are included only if the organiser confirms them in writing.
14. Do not connect electrical appliances, use open flames, hazardous materials, loud equipment, or generators without prior written venue approval. Keep cables and equipment safely secured.
15. Display prices clearly and describe products honestly. You are responsible for taking payments from your customers and handling customer questions or complaints about your products.
16. Do not sell counterfeit, illegal, unsafe, restricted, or age-controlled products. The organiser may ask you to remove any item that violates venue rules or applicable law.
17. You are responsible for damage or loss caused by you, your staff, equipment, or products. Report accidents, damage, hazards, or lost property to event staff promptly.
18. Keep your stall staffed during the agreed operating hours. If you need to leave temporarily, arrange responsible cover and inform the event organiser.
19. The event may be delayed, moved, changed, or interrupted by safety issues, severe weather, venue restrictions, or other circumstances outside the organiser’s reasonable control. The organiser will share important changes through the contact details you provide.
20. Do not use CultureWave, event, venue, or other stall-holder names, logos, or photos in advertising without permission. Obtain any consent required before photographing or recording identifiable attendees.

Refund policy

If your application is rejected, the stall fee will be refunded to the original payment method. Once accepted, the fee is non-refundable if you cancel or do not attend. If the organiser cancels the event, the stall fee will be refunded to the original payment method. Refund timing depends on the payment provider and your bank. Any refund will be for the stall fee paid; your bank or payment provider may apply its own processing timelines.

Privacy and contact

Your contact and stall details will be shared with the event organiser to review and manage your application. CultureWave may contact you about payment, application status, or event arrangements. Your information will be used for stall application administration and related event communications. Contact CultureWave through the support details on the website if you need to correct your information or ask about your application.

Acceptance

By selecting the agreement checkbox and proceeding to payment, you confirm that you have read and understood these terms, that your stall does not sell food or drinks, and that you agree to follow the event organiser’s reasonable instructions.
