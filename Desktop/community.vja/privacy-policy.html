Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the current Community website handles, what stays in your browser, and what to expect if a live service is added.

Effective: October 15, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 15, 2026

Important: this is a front-end preview

The current login and booking forms do not send or store the details you type. The booking form does not make a reservation or take payment. This page describes the present implementation; it must be revised before connecting accounts, analytics, booking, messaging, or payment services.

1. Who is responsible

The Community site is presented as owned by OGSHOOTS LUXE and developed by Srisanth Boddu. In this policy, “Community,” “we,” and “our” refer to this site and its operator. For privacy questions, use the contact details in the Contact section.

2. What happens in the current preview

  • The booking page validates the name, email, phone number, event, and ticket quantity in your browser, then displays a preview message. It does not submit those values to a server or save them in browser storage.
  • The login page validates a phone number locally and displays a preview message. It does not send a verification code or create an account.
  • On the rebuilt home, explore, clubs, booking, login, and policy pages, the city button changes the displayed city label without saving it. The older membership page saves the selected city in local storage under community-vja-city.
  • The theme control saves your light or dark preference in your browser’s local storage under community-theme.
  • The app QR graphic and store buttons are visual placeholders and do not initiate a download or encode a live booking.
  • No analytics or advertising script is included in this preview’s page code.

Information held only in an open form may remain visible in that browser tab until you clear it or close the page. The site code does not retain those form entries after that.

3. Information that may be involved in a connected service

If features are connected in the future, the information requested will depend on what you choose to use. It may include:

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address, selected city, profile details, and account identifiers.
  • Booking details: event, date and time, ticket type and quantity, attendee names where required, booking reference, attendance, cancellation, refund, and support history.
  • Payment references: order amount, payment status, payment-provider transaction reference, refund status, and limited payment method details needed to reconcile a transaction. Payment credentials should be handled by the payment provider, not sent through a message or support request.
  • Communications and content: messages sent to support and information you choose to submit through a review, profile, event, or community feature.
  • Technical information: basic request, device, browser, or diagnostic details that the hosting or service providers process to deliver, secure, and maintain a connected service.

The current preview does not collect the form or account information above through a backend.

4. How information may be used

If and when a live feature is introduced, information may be used to provide that feature: create and secure an account; confirm and manage a booking; send event or service updates; support a refund or complaint; prevent fraud or abuse; maintain platform security; and meet legal, tax, or recordkeeping duties. Optional communications should be used only under the choices and permissions presented to you.

The current preview uses the city selection to change on-page text and the theme preference to restore your visual theme. It does not use booking form entries for these purposes.

5. When information may be shared

The current front-end code does not send booking or login form entries to Community, event organisers, or payment providers. If a connected booking service is later introduced, the minimum details needed to fulfil a reservation may be shared with the relevant event organiser, venue, payment provider, or customer-support provider. The information shown at booking time should explain who receives it and why.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect users and the security of the service, subject to applicable law.

6. External content and service providers

Some pages request font files from Google Fonts and experience-card images from Unsplash. When your browser loads those assets, the provider may receive ordinary request metadata such as your IP address and browser information. The Community preview does not append your booking-form values to those asset requests. These providers operate under their own privacy terms and technical practices.

Links to app stores, event organisers, venues, maps, or other external services may take you to services Community does not control. Review their privacy information before submitting data there.

7. Payments

No payment details are requested or processed by the current preview. The sample ticket prices are display content only. If payments are connected, the checkout should identify the payment provider and explain which transaction details Community receives. Never provide your password, one-time password, UPI PIN, or complete card credentials to Community support.

8. Cookies, browser storage, and retention

The current site code does not set cookies. It saves the visual theme under community-theme; the older membership page also saves the chosen city under community-vja-city. You can remove these preferences by clearing this site’s local storage in your browser. The page will then use its default theme and city.

The form entries are not written to local storage by the current page code. A web host may independently keep basic server logs needed to operate or protect the website; the hosting configuration and its retention period are not defined by this front-end project. If a live account or booking database is added, a retention schedule will be published and data will be kept only as long as needed for the stated service, legal records, and dispute handling.

9. Security

The preview keeps booking and login form values in the open browser form and does not transmit them through its page scripts. No online system can promise absolute security. Use a trusted device and network, close the page if you are sharing a device, and do not send sensitive authentication or payment credentials by email or message.

Before any live service is launched, its account, booking, and payment integrations should use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards and a process for handling security incidents.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to enter details into the preview forms. You can clear the saved theme setting in your browser. If a connected service holds account or booking information about you, contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of an optional permission where applicable. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where required by law or necessary to resolve a booking dispute.

11. Children and family events

Some event listings may be suitable for families, but a child’s participation is governed by the event organiser’s age and supervision rules. A parent or legal guardian should provide any information required for a child’s booking and should not submit a child’s information unless authorised to do so. The current preview does not create child profiles or collect children’s data through a backend.

12. Processing outside your region

Google Fonts and Unsplash assets are served by external providers and may be delivered through infrastructure outside your location. Any future account, booking, or payment service may also use providers in other regions. Before enabling those integrations, this policy should name the relevant providers and describe applicable safeguards and transfer practices.

13. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when site features, service providers, or data practices change. The latest revision date appears at the top. If a change materially affects information already held in a connected service, we will provide any notice or choice required by applicable law.

14. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, corrections, or requests, email hello@community.in. Include enough detail for us to understand your request, but do not include passwords, one-time codes, UPI PINs, or full payment credentials.

The current preview has no account database to search and cannot fulfil account-data requests for records it does not hold. If live services are added, the operator’s grievance contact and process should be published here.
