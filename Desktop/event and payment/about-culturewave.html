Our story

Made for the moments outside the routine.

Some of the best memories start with a simple question: "What should we do?" CultureWave was built to make that answer easier — whether you're looking for a night out, a new place to eat, a creative workshop or a reason to celebrate.

We started in Vijayawada with a vision to make premium cultural experiences accessible to everyone. Today we power events across India, giving organizers the tools to share their passion and guests the confidence to show up.

We're part of OGSHOOTS LUXE — a creative studio that believes in building things that last.
