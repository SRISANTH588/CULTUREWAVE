Required documents

Prepare these items before you start. Upload clear, readable copies during the identity documents step.

Required for every vendor

  • ✓PAN details and PAN image
    Enter the 10-character PAN and upload a PNG or JPG image, up to 1 MB.
  • ✓Aadhaar details and Aadhaar image
    Enter the 12-digit Aadhaar number and upload a PNG or JPG image, up to 1 MB. Mask the first eight digits where possible.
  • ✓Payout bank details
    Have the account number, IFSC, beneficiary name and account type ready. These are entered in the form.
  • ✓Business and contact details
    Registered business name, business type, category, address, primary contact and verified email.
  • ✓Tax declarations and consent
    Answer the GSTIN and ITR questions and accept the vendor terms and required partner policies.

Additional supporting documents can be attached if they help verify your business. Uploads should be clear and readable.

Optional supporting documents

  • ＋Business registration or incorporation certificate
  • ＋GST certificate, address proof, bank proof, or MSME / Udyam certificate
  • ＋Other documents relevant to your business or selected category

File preparation

  • iAccepted formats: PNG, JPG or JPEG.
  • iKeep each image at or below 1 MB and make sure names, numbers and photo details are legible.
  • iUse the registered vendor or proprietor details consistently across the application.
