Gift Card FAQs

Answers to common questions about CultureWave Gift Cards.

CultureWave Gift Cards are prepaid digital cards issued by Pine Labs and Razorpay. Once claimed, they can be used to pay for eligible orders on CultureWave.
Each gift card is valid for 1 year from the date it is redeemed.
A single Gift Card denomination must be at least ₹250 and cannot exceed ₹10,000.
Open the hamburger menu on CultureWave, select Gift Cards, then choose Claim Gift Card. Enter the 16-digit code and 6-digit PIN. Open Claim Gift Card.

Note: Claimed gift card balance is added to your CultureWave balance. The maximum balance that can be held is ₹25,000 at any time.
After a successful claim, the Gift Card amount is added to your CultureWave balance and can be used to pay for eligible event tickets and dining orders on CultureWave.
Your available balance is applied during checkout on eligible orders. You can unselect the balance option at checkout if you do not want to use it for that order.
You can add up to ₹25,000 per month and hold a maximum CultureWave balance of ₹25,000.
Email support.culturewave@gmail.com. Include the phone number registered to your CultureWave account and your Gift Card query. Do not email your gift card PIN.
