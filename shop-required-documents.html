Shop partner application

Required documents

Prepare the identity, business and payout documents for your shop. Food shops have extra food safety requirements.

Required for every shop

  • ✓PAN details and PAN copy
    Enter the 10-character PAN and upload a PNG/JPG image up to 1 MB.
  • ✓Aadhaar details and Aadhaar copy
    Enter the 12-digit number and upload a PNG/JPG image up to 1 MB. Mask the first eight digits where possible.
  • ✓Shop establishment or trade license
    Upload a clear PNG/JPG up to 1 MB or PDF up to 400 KB.
  • ✓Bank proof
    Upload a cancelled cheque or bank statement showing the account holder and number. PNG/JPG up to 1 MB or PDF up to 400 KB.
  • ✓Payout bank details
    Enter account number, IFSC, beneficiary name and account type.
  • ✓Shop and contact information
    Registered name, business type, category, address, primary contact and verified email.

Required when applicable

  • ✓GST certificate
    Required if you provide a GSTIN. Enter a valid GSTIN and upload the registration certificate (PNG/JPG up to 1 MB or PDF up to 400 KB).
  • ✓FSSAI license or registration for food shops
    Groceries, prepared food, specialty pantry, bakery and produce shops must provide the 14-digit number, expiry date and current certificate. The certificate must be unexpired. PNG/JPG up to 1 MB or PDF up to 400 KB.
