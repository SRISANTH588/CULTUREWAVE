Guides
Step-by-step help for common tasks.
🎟️

How to book a ticket

Find an event → select ticket type → enter your details → pay securely → get your QR ticket instantly in My Bookings.

💸

Requesting a refund

Open My Bookings → tap Cancel → confirm via WhatsApp. Refunds take 5–7 business days after admin approval.

📋

Creating an event

Go to Create Event → fill in details, venue, date, and ticket tiers → submit for review. Your event goes live within 24 hours.

📲

Checking in attendees

Open the Check-in tool → scan the attendee's QR code or search by booking ID → mark as checked in with one tap.

👤

Managing your account

Log in → go to My Profile to view bookings, update contact details, or log out securely from any device.

🔔

Staying updated

Check the Notifications tab in your dashboard for booking confirmations, event reminders, and updates from organisers.
