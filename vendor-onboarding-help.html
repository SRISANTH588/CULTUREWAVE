Vendor onboarding guide

Get your lister account ready.

Here’s what to prepare before you begin your CultureWave vendor application, and what happens after you submit it.

01

Enter your vendor details

Provide the registered vendor name and address, city, GST and ITR details, bank payout information, and a contact name, email and mobile number. The LoU e-sign invitation is sent to the contact email.

02

Add identity documents

Enter the vendor’s PAN and Aadhaar numbers and upload clear images of both cards. Supporting documents are optional. Uploads must be PNG or JPEG images up to 1 MB each.

03

Review before submitting

Check spelling, account number and IFSC carefully. Make sure each image is readable and that the details belong to the vendor applying.

04

Wait for team review

After submission, your application is marked as pending review. CultureWave will contact you using the contact details supplied if more information is needed.

Common questions

Quick answers about the application and documents.

Can I apply if I don’t have a GSTIN?

Yes. Select “No” when asked whether you have a GSTIN. The GST number field is only required when you select “Yes.”

Are PAN and Aadhaar required?

Yes. Enter both PAN and Aadhaar numbers, then upload a clear image of each card. Supporting documents remain optional.

Which files can I upload?

PAN, Aadhaar and additional documents accept PNG, JPG or JPEG image files up to 1 MB per file. For privacy, mask the first eight digits on an Aadhaar image where possible.

Does entering my mobile number verify it?

No. The vendor onboarding page currently collects a contact number but does not send or verify an OTP.

Why can’t I finish submitting sensitive documents?

Secure server-side document submission is still being set up. The page currently validates the form in your browser but does not upload PAN, Aadhaar or bank details. Contact CultureWave to complete onboarding until the secure submission endpoint is available.

How do I update an application I already sent?

Contact the CultureWave team and include the vendor name and contact email used in the application so they can help locate it.

Need help with your application?

Include your vendor name and contact number so we can help with the right next step.
